## Differentiation opportunities (product hypotheses, not proven wins)

1. Deterministic Big Five scoring with public-domain items and published reverse-key rules.
2. Honest “not a percentile” score display.
3. Original archetypes labeled as product constructs, not fake science.
4. Mixed profiles instead of forced types.
5. Continuous practice (journal, habits, programs) after the test.
6. Coach that cites measured vs user-provided vs educational.
7. Crisis protocol that refuses to play therapist.
8. Journal privacy with separate AI authorization.
9. Consent-based relationship comparison that excludes journals.
10. Explicit ban on hiring/firing automation.
11. Enterprise voluntary development with individual result firewall.
12. Open scoring algorithm version + response hash on reports.
13. Mini-IPIP + IPIP-50 ladder rather than a single viral quiz.
14. Dark/light accessible design tokens, not a competitor clone.
15. Freemium that still gives a complete Big Five, not a bait-and-switch 5% teaser — **ethical conversion hypothesis**.
16. Admin AI cost monitoring (architecture ready; production telemetry pending).
17. Multilingual framework with a ban on assuming translated items stay valid.
18. Shareable card that does not leak scores-as-percentiles.
19. Development programs measured in days of practice, not type lore.
20. Scientific governance docs in-repo, including what is *not* validated.

## What we will not copy

Myers-Briggs proprietary items, competitor report prose, O*NET Interest Profiler item text (separate license), or clinical diagnostic language.
