# Market research — PersonalityDNA AI

**Retrieved:** 2026-11-26  
**Limitation:** Public marketing pages and third-party roundups. No primary user interviews, no purchased market reports, no invented TAM/SAM/download figures.

## Category

Personality assessment and personal-development software for adults. Adjacent categories: coaching marketplaces, HR talent tools, strengths assessments, typology entertainment sites.

## Demand signals (qualitative)

- Free personality tests are used as top-of-funnel content (16Personalities, IDRlabs, HIGH5).
- Paid conversion typically happens via reports, “suites,” courses, or coaching — not the first score screen.
- Enterprise buyers (BetterUp, Predictive Index) sell workforce products that this product **explicitly refuses to automate** (hiring/firing). PersonalityDNA’s enterprise portal is voluntary development only.

## Assumptions (unverified)

- A scientifically careful Big Five product can differentiate against typology entertainment — assumption, not proven.
- Users will return for coaching/programs after the first test — hypothesis requiring retention data.
- $9.99 / $69.99 price points are hypotheses copied from the product brief, not validated.
