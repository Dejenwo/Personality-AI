# Privacy requirements

Personality assessments, journals, and coaching are treated as sensitive personal information.

Implemented:

- Privacy consent required at registration
- Consent ledger with revoke timestamps
- Private-by-default profiles
- Journal AI opt-in per entry and in preferences
- Data export JSON
- Account deletion request workflow
- Relationship comparison excludes journals and coaching
- No sale of personality data in product policy copy

Outstanding before launch: formal GDPR/CCPA legal review, DPA, retention jobs, subprocessors list, cookie banner if analytics are added, encryption-at-rest evidence.
